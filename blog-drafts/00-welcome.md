# Welcome: real stories from ordinary people on the road to financial independence

Most of what's written about financial independence is numbers. Save this much, invest it like that, withdraw 4% a year, done. The numbers matter, and this site has calculators for all of them. But numbers rarely change how someone lives.

Stories do.

## What this blog is

This blog collects **FIRE stories from all over the web**: ordinary people sharing, often anonymously, how they took control of their money and bought back their time.

These aren't celebrities or famous bloggers. They're teachers, librarians, government workers, engineers and single parents. Some retired in their thirties on less than most people spend on rent. Some built eight-figure portfolios on salaries that never looked spectacular. Some are still on the way, and some reached their number and found that quitting was the hardest part.

The point isn't to copy anyone. It's to see what's possible, find someone whose situation looks a bit like yours, and borrow the ideas that fit your own life.

## Where the stories come from

Most stories start in online communities, like r/financialindependence, r/Fire, r/leanfire and r/fatFIRE on Reddit, where people share their numbers openly because they can do it anonymously.

We **retell each story in our own words** and **link to where it was reported**, so you can dig deeper. We deliberately **leave out names and usernames**: these are private people, and the lessons don't depend on who they are. Numbers are as the person reported them. Nobody can check a stranger's brokerage account, so read every story as one person's experience, not a guarantee.

## How every story is told

To make the stories easy to compare, they all follow the same structure:

- **The snapshot:** age, household, income, savings rate and portfolio, at a glance
- **The starting point:** where they were when they began
- **The turning point:** the moment something clicked
- **The plan:** what they actually did
- **The numbers:** the key figures
- **The hard parts:** setbacks, doubts and mistakes
- **Life after FI:** what changed, and what didn't
- **Lessons worth stealing:** the ideas you can use yourself
- **How to do this in the US:** the accounts, tax rules and health insurance options that make the story work, from 401(k)s and Roth IRAs to the ACA and Social Security

## Use the stories with the tools

When a story inspires you, try it on your own numbers:

- Find your FI number and your timeline with the [calculators](index.html#calculators)
- See which of your costs your savings already cover in the [tracker](tracker.html). Every $1,000 invested pays for $40 a year under the 4% rule, for good.

## A word of caution

Nothing here is financial, tax or legal advice. Every story is one person's path, shaped by their income, their state, their luck and their timing. Take the inspiration, check the details against your own situation, and do your own math.
