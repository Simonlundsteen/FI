# Welcome: real stories from the road to financial independence

Most of what's written about financial independence is numbers. Save this much, invest it like that, withdraw 4% a year, done. The numbers matter, and this site has calculators for all of them. But numbers rarely change how someone lives.

Stories do.

## What this blog is

This blog collects **FIRE stories from all over the web**: real people sharing how they took control of their money and bought back their time. Some retired in their thirties. Some cut back to part-time at fifty. Some are still on the way and honest about the bumps.

You'll meet people with modest incomes and people with big ones. You'll meet frugal minimalists and people who refused to give up the things they love. People in big cities and in small towns. People who did everything right and people who got there after expensive mistakes.

The point isn't to copy anyone. It's to see what's possible, find someone whose situation looks a bit like yours, and borrow the ideas that fit your own life.

## Where the stories come from

Most stories start as posts in online communities, like the FIRE groups on Reddit, along with blogs and forums where people share their journeys openly.

We **retell each story in our own words** and always **link back to the original**, so you can read it in the author's own voice and see the discussion around it. Numbers are as the person reported them. We can't check anyone's bank account, so read them as one person's experience, not a guarantee.

## How every story is told

To make the stories easy to compare, they all follow the same structure:

- **The snapshot:** age, household, income, savings rate, and how long it took, at a glance
- **The starting point:** where they were when they began
- **The turning point:** the moment something clicked
- **The plan:** what they actually did, step by step
- **The numbers:** how the net worth grew over time
- **The hard parts:** setbacks, doubts and mistakes
- **Life after FI:** what changed, and what didn't
- **Lessons worth stealing:** the ideas you can use yourself
- **What it would look like in Denmark:** many stories come from other countries, so we translate the key numbers to kroner and point out where Danish taxes, pensions and healthcare change the picture

## Use the stories with the tools

When a story inspires you, try it on your own numbers:

- Find your FI number and your timeline with the [calculators](index.html#calculators)
- See which costs in your life your savings already cover in the [tracker](tracker.html). At 10,000 kr. invested, the 4% rule pays for a brunch for two every year, for good.
- Keep an eye on the big picture on your [dashboard](dashboard.html)

## A word of caution

Nothing here is financial advice. Every story is one person's path, shaped by their country, their tax rules, their luck and their timing. Take the inspiration, check the details against your own situation, and do your own math.
