Every milestone pays for something, for life.

By the 4% rule, every amount you invest can pay for a yearly cost of 4% of that amount, for good. At 10,000 you can afford 400 a year, like a brunch for two, without ever touching the principal. Enter your net worth, name what each milestone pays for, and watch your life get funded piece by piece.

4% is the classic rule. Use 3–3.5% to be safer.
